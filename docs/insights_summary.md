# Insights Summary

## Overview

This document summarizes the main business insights found in the **DiscoverIQ — Ecommerce Product Discovery & Conversion Analytics Platform** project.

The project analyzed October and November 2019 ecommerce behavior data to understand product discovery, conversion drop-offs, product ranking opportunities, category and brand performance, and user segment behavior.

The core finding is:

> November had more traffic and stronger product interest, but revenue declined because purchase completion became much weaker.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

## Main Business Story

October represented more normal ecommerce behavior.

November showed a clear pre-Black-Friday behavior shift:

```text
More traffic
More browsing
More cart activity
Lower purchase completion
Higher cart abandonment
Lower revenue
```

This means November did not fail because users stopped visiting the store.

It failed because users did not complete purchases after showing product interest.

---

## Key Numbers

| Metric              | October | November |     Change |
| ------------------- | ------: | -------: | ---------: |
| Sessions            |   3.12M |    3.43M |     +9.70% |
| Users               |   1.50M |    1.60M |     +6.89% |
| Revenue             | $27.02M |  $20.36M |    -24.65% |
| Conversion Rate     |   2.82% |    2.05% |  -0.77 pts |
| Cart Abandonment    |  13.68% |   67.73% | +54.05 pts |
| Revenue per Session |   $8.65 |    $5.94 |     -$2.71 |

## Insight

November brought more traffic, but not better revenue.

The business problem was conversion quality, especially the collapse from cart to purchase.

---

# 2. Product Discovery Funnel Insight

## Funnel Performance

| Funnel Stage          | October | November |
| --------------------- | ------: | -------: |
| Product View Sessions |   3.02M |    3.26M |
| Cart Sessions         |   98.7K |   206.8K |
| Purchase Sessions     |   85.2K |    66.7K |

## Funnel Rates

| Metric                | October | November |
| --------------------- | ------: | -------: |
| View-to-Cart Rate     |   3.27% |    6.35% |
| Cart-to-Purchase Rate |  86.32% |   32.27% |
| View-to-Purchase Rate |   2.82% |    2.05% |

## Insight

November had stronger product interest because view-to-cart rate improved.

However, cart-to-purchase conversion dropped sharply.

This means users were willing to add products to cart, but many did not complete the purchase.

## Business Interpretation

The issue was not early product discovery.

The issue was late-stage purchase hesitation.

Possible reasons:

* users waiting for Black Friday deals,
* price comparison behavior,
* checkout hesitation,
* weak urgency signals,
* delivery or trust concerns,
* lack of personalized offers,
* cart used as a wishlist.

---

# 3. Cart Abandonment Was the Biggest Problem

## Observation

Cart abandonment increased from:

```text
13.68% in October → 67.73% in November
```

This was the most important negative change in the project.

## Insight

Users were adding products to cart at a higher rate in November, but they were not buying.

This strongly suggests pre-sale browsing and waiting behavior.

## Business Action

The ecommerce business should focus on reducing cart abandonment through:

* abandoned cart recovery,
* personalized reminders,
* urgency messages,
* price-drop alerts,
* limited-time offers,
* checkout simplification,
* delivery and return clarity,
* trust-building product page content.

---

# 4. Revenue Declined Despite More Traffic

## Observation

November sessions increased by around 9.70%, but revenue dropped by around 24.65%.

## Insight

More traffic does not automatically mean better business performance.

If conversion quality drops, higher traffic can still produce lower revenue.

## Business Interpretation

The store attracted more users in November, but those users were less purchase-ready.

This is common during promotional periods when shoppers browse early, compare prices, and wait for discounts.

---

# 5. Electronics Was the Dominant Category but Caused the Largest Decline

## Category Revenue

| Category     | October Revenue | November Revenue |  Change |
| ------------ | --------------: | ---------------: | ------: |
| Electronics  |         $20.73M |          $15.14M | -$5.60M |
| Appliances   |          $1.63M |           $1.40M |  -$226K |
| Construction |           $113K |             $87K |   -$26K |

## Insight

Electronics was the largest revenue category in both months.

However, it also caused the biggest revenue decline.

## Business Interpretation

Electronics had strong demand and visibility, but purchase completion weakened in November.

This suggests that many users were browsing electronics and adding products to cart but delaying purchases.

## Business Action

For electronics, the business should test:

* stronger discount communication,
* urgency banners,
* product comparison features,
* personalized recommendations,
* better ranking of high-converting products,
* promotional bundles,
* price-drop alerts.

# 7. Product Ranking Insight

## Product Ranking Question

The product ranking page answers:

> Are the right products receiving the right visibility?

Products were grouped into four action segments:

| Segment                                      | Meaning                                           | Business Action        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Winner Product                               | High visibility and high conversion               | Keep ranking high      |
| Hidden Gem - Promote Higher                  | Low or moderate visibility with strong conversion | Promote higher         |
| High Visibility Low Conversion - Investigate | High visibility but weak conversion               | Review or deprioritize |
| Low Priority Product                         | Low visibility and weak conversion                | Low focus              |

---

## Hidden-Gem Products

Hidden gems were defined as products with:

```text
20 to 200 views
conversion rate >= 3%
total purchases >= 3
```

## Insight

Some products had low visibility but strong conversion.

These products are underexposed.

## Business Action

Hidden-gem products should be promoted through:

* higher ranking,
* recommendation sections,
* category page placement,
* campaign exposure,
* “similar products” modules,
* personalized product suggestions.

---

## High-Visibility Low-Conversion Products

Weak products were defined as products with:

```text
1,000+ views
conversion rate < 1%
less than 10 purchases
```

## Insight

Some products received strong visibility but failed to convert.

These products may be wasting ranking space.

## Business Action

High-visibility low-conversion products should be investigated for:

* price mismatch,
* poor product images,
* weak product descriptions,
* irrelevant ranking,
* low trust,
* bad reviews,
* wrong audience,
* poor offer quality.

The business should either fix these products or reduce their ranking priority.

# 8. User Segment Insight

## User Segment Revenue per User

| Segment   | October Revenue/User | November Revenue/User |
| --------- | -------------------: | --------------------: |
| New       |                $6.64 |                 $5.55 |
| Returning |               $23.06 |                $16.90 |
| Loyal     |              $105.71 |                $55.92 |

## Insight

Loyal users generated the highest revenue per user.

However, loyal user spending dropped sharply in November.

## Business Interpretation

Even the most engaged users reduced spending in November.

This supports the idea that users were waiting for better deals or delaying purchase decisions.

## Business Action

The business should protect loyal users with:

* personalized offers,
* early access deals,
* loyalty rewards,
* price-drop alerts,
* relevant product recommendations,
* cart recovery emails,
* exclusive bundles.

# 9. Time Pattern Insight

## Peak Behavior

| Metric             | October   | November |
| ------------------ | --------- | -------- |
| Peak Purchase Hour | 9:00 AM   | 9:00 AM  |
| Best Day           | Wednesday | Sunday   |
| Worst Day          | Monday    | Thursday |

## Insight

October behavior was more weekday-driven.

November behavior shifted toward weekend shopping.

## Business Interpretation

November users likely had more time to browse during weekends, especially around promotional periods.

## Business Action

The business can schedule campaigns, recommendations, and reminder messages around stronger shopping windows.

For November-like promotional periods, Sunday can be a high-priority campaign day.

# 10. Co-Purchase Insight

## Observation

Multi-purchase sessions declined in November.

| Pair Type                 | October | November |
| ------------------------- | ------: | -------: |
| Electronics + Electronics |     757 |      395 |
| Appliances + Appliances   |      87 |       66 |
| Electronics + Appliances  |      53 |       56 |

## Insight

Users bought fewer product combinations in November.

This suggests users were more cautious and less likely to build larger baskets before the major sale period.

## Business Action

The business can increase basket size through:

* frequently bought together modules,
* bundle recommendations,
* cart-based cross-sell,
* accessory suggestions,
* “complete your setup” recommendations.

---

# 11. Weekly Trend Insight

## Observation

November had a strong revenue spike during the Black Friday window, but conversion remained weak.

## Insight

High traffic and high revenue can happen at the same time as weak conversion.

This means many users were browsing heavily, but only a smaller percentage were purchasing.

## Business Action

During promotional peaks, the business should focus on:

* converting high-intent users faster,
* showing urgency signals,
* reducing checkout friction,
* ranking high-converting products,
* promoting bundles,
* retargeting cart abandoners.

# 13. Product Discovery Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the ecommerce business should prioritize the following actions.

## 1. Reduce Cart Abandonment

Cart abandonment was the biggest problem in November.

Recommended actions:

* abandoned cart reminders,
* urgency signals,
* limited-time offers,
* personalized discounts,
* simplified checkout,
* clearer delivery and return messaging.

## 2. Promote Hidden Gems

Underexposed high-converting products should receive better placement.

Recommended actions:

* boost in search results,
* recommendation modules,
* category page promotion,
* personalized placements.

## 3. Investigate High-Visibility Low-Conversion Products

Products with high visibility and weak conversion should be reviewed.

Recommended actions:

* check pricing,
* improve images,
* improve descriptions,
* review ranking rules,
* check product relevance,
* compare against substitutes.

## 4. Optimize Electronics

Electronics was the biggest revenue driver and the biggest source of decline.

Recommended actions:

* focus conversion optimization on electronics first,
* improve ranking within electronics,
* promote high-converting electronics products,
* reduce friction for premium electronics purchases.

## 5. Personalize for Loyal Users

Loyal users have the highest value.

Recommended actions:

* early access offers,
* loyalty campaigns,
* personalized recommendations,
* exclusive bundles,
* high-intent cart recovery.

# 14. Final Business Story

The complete dashboard story is:

```text
October showed stronger purchase completion.

November had more users, more sessions, and stronger early product interest.

However, users were less ready to purchase.

Cart abandonment increased sharply.

Revenue declined even though traffic increased.

The biggest opportunity is to improve cart-to-purchase conversion, promote hidden gems, reduce wasted product visibility, and personalize experiences for high-value users.
```

---

# 15. Final Conclusion

DiscoverIQ shows that ecommerce performance cannot be judged by traffic alone.

A business can increase sessions, product views, and cart activity but still lose revenue if users do not complete purchases.

The project found that November’s main problem was not product discovery volume. It was purchase completion quality.

The most important business actions are:

1. reduce cart abandonment,
2. improve checkout and urgency messaging,
3. promote hidden-gem products,
4. investigate high-visibility low-conversion products,
5. optimize electronics and top brands,
6. personalize experiences for loyal and returning users.

These actions connect analytics directly to ecommerce growth, product discovery optimization, and conversion improvement.
